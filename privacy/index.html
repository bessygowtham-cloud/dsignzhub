Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Dsignzhub ("we", "us") handles information collected through https://www.dsignzhub.com (this "site") and when you contact us directly. Dsignzhub is a digital agency based in India, offering website design and development, e-commerce, SEO, Google Ads, branding and related services.

Information we collect

  • Contact form. When you submit the form on our Contact page, we collect your name, email address, phone number, company name (if provided), the service you're interested in, and your message. This is sent to our inbox to respond to your enquiry.
  • WhatsApp and phone. If you click the WhatsApp button or our phone number, you leave this site to open WhatsApp or your phone's dialler directly — we don't collect anything from that click itself, though any conversation you then have with us is handled the same as any other business enquiry.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our hosting provider's servers log standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability purposes. This site does not currently set any cookies of its own.

How we use this information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide quotes or information you've requested
  • To deliver services you've engaged us for
  • To maintain ordinary business and financial records

We do not sell your information to third parties, and we don't use it for purposes unrelated to the above without asking you first.

Third-party services

  • Web3Forms processes contact form submissions on our behalf so they reach our inbox. See their privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on this site from Google's servers, which may log standard technical data such as your IP address as part of delivering the font files.
  • This site is hosted across GitHub Pages and a MilesWeb server, each of which may log standard access data as part of normal hosting operation.

If we start using analytics or advertising tools that set cookies (for example, Google Analytics or Google Ads), we'll update this policy and add a cookie consent notice before doing so.

Data retention

We keep enquiry and project information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, deliver services, and meet our own legal and accounting obligations, after which it's deleted or anonymised.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information you share with us, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable law, you can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. To do this, email us at hello@dsignzhub.com and we'll respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Children's privacy

This site and our services are intended for businesses and individuals who can enter into a contract, and are not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if we start using new tools that collect data. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest revision.

Questions about this policy? Email hello@dsignzhub.com.
